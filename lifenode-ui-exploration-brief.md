# Lifenode UI Exploration

Work directly from the existing Lifenode codebase and explore the strongest visual direction for the product before changing the interface.

Lifenode is a life-planning game on a free-form node canvas: a player runs multiple life decisions in parallel, watches their shared resources collide, and receives an absurdist, deadpan account of the outcome. It is closer to building a case against your own future than using a productivity tool.

Evolve the UI so the product feels distinctive, deliberate, and enjoyable to inhabit. Preserve the clarity required to build and execute a graph, but let the interaction model, visual hierarchy, density, atmosphere, and moments of feedback grow from the product's actual premise and existing implementation.

Do not settle for generic AI-app aesthetics, polished dashboard defaults, decorative gradients, interchangeable cards, or superficial restyling. Avoid visual noise, fake complexity, and features that do not strengthen the act of planning a life and seeing its consequences.

Inspect the current flows, components, and constraints first. Then make coherent product-design decisions that fit Lifenode specifically, carry them through the relevant states, and implement only changes that genuinely improve the experience. Keep the existing behaviour correct and the node canvas practical to use.
